The Sedi Laka approach

Come as you are. We begin there.

You do not need to arrive with the right words or a complete plan. We begin by making space for your experience, then work together at a pace that respects your circumstances.

01

Pause

Find enough safety and steadiness to notice what you are carrying, without pressure to solve it all at once.

02

Understand

Explore the patterns, feelings and needs beneath the overwhelm. Naming what is happening can make room for clarity.

03

Move with intention

Choose practical next steps that fit your life, while leaving room to reflect, adapt and begin again.

A note from Sarah

“As long as you are alive, there is hope.”

I’m Sarah Kihuha, a trauma therapist and therapeutic coach. I founded Sedi Laka Center to offer a calm, nonjudgmental space where people can reconnect with themselves and their capacity to move forward.

Our name means “my light.” Sometimes the work begins by remembering that your light is still there, even in a difficult season.

Clarity Package

US$475 / five sessions

Five weekly private sessions to make space for understanding and focused work.

Integration Package

US$900 / ten sessions

Ten private sessions over a maximum of three months for sustained work and integration.

At the end of a package, we review your progress together. You can continue with individual sessions, choose a suitable package, pause or conclude the work.

Vision Board Experience

A year of shared intention.

Begin at the start-of-year Vision Board Brunch, a guided gathering to shape your vision for the year. After the brunch, you can choose whether to continue with a small, intimate group for the rest of the year.

01

Begin together

Join the Vision Board Brunch. The group has no more than 15 participants and closes to new members after February, so the space can remain familiar and private.

02

Keep showing up

For those who continue: one virtual group session each month, ongoing group connection, and a retreat each quarter for reflection, accountability and encouragement.

03

Celebrate the year

Close with an end-of-year celebration of what you have discovered and built together. You are free to leave the group at any point.

This is a group coaching experience, not group therapy. Dates, participation details and fees will be announced here.
